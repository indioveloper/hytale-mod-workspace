# Third-party assets

The nine color textures and temporary inventory icons in this pack are copied
from Metalwork 1.0.0 by Lordimass, distributed under GPL-3.0-only.

- Source supplied by the user: `Metalwork-main.zip`
- Upstream author: Lordimass
- Upstream project: Metalwork
- License: GPL-3.0-only

The flat-roof block model in this pack is newly authored for 32x32 textures.
The item definitions inherit the public runtime behavior of Hytale's
`Rock_Stone_Brick_Roof_Flat` asset; no vanilla model or texture is copied into
the repository.
